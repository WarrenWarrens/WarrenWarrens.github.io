I'm Ryan, a programmer, computer engineer, lifelong learner and the type of curious to constantly explore rabbit holes, side projects and experiments.

Based in Toronto, Canada. Working on my Masters at Ontario Tech University, while working part-time as a day receiver at Princess Auto.

Professionally, i build myself on the core principles of making and improving software to be as easy to use, customizable, accessible and a product people actually want to use.

When i'm not studying but still feeling productive, you can find me working on personal experiments and side projects ranging from visual arts with ink and water colours, to crafting electronics with a soldering iron in one hand, and writing code in the other.

When i need a break from being productive, i love to simply unwind with playing video games, movies, reading, cooking, bouldering and weight lifting.

Here are some of my favourites: Donnie Darko, Oppenheimer, Death Grips, Persona 4 Golden, Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty, Jojo's Bizarre Adventure & Slipknot.
